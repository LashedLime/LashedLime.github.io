A lightweight Minecraft launcher that stays out of your game

Sign in, install Minecraft and Fabric, manage mods, and launch from one place. Orvex is designed to use minimal resources so your PC can focus on gaming.

Download Learn more

No ads or telemetry. Your game files stay separate from the standard .minecraft folder.

What it does

Everything you need to get into Minecraft, without unnecessary background load.

Secure sign-in

Sign in securely with your Microsoft account. Your password is never handled by Orvex.

Fully self-contained

Minecraft, Fabric, assets, and Java are installed in Orvex’s own game directory, separate from your existing setup.

Lightweight by design

Orvex keeps its idle resource use low, leaving more of your PC available for Minecraft.

One-click repair

Repair missing or corrupted launcher files with one click—no manual cleanup required.
